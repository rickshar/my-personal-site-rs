# Product Documentation at Tomia

I worked at [Tomia](https://tomiaglobal.com/network-roaming) (previously known as Starhome, and Starhome MACH) from 2007-2019.

In my earlier, pre-managerial role, I worked on a wide variety of mobile telephony roaming applications, including Intelligent Preferred Network (IPN - SoR), Optimal Voicemail Deposit (OVMD), Roaming Control, Local Roaming Number (LRN), and Gateway Location Register (GLR).

In my latter years at Tomia, I documented the campaign management Sparx product in addition to my managerial and team-leading duties.

The Tomia documentation is hidden behind a gateway and is only accessible to Tomia customers and internal functions. The theory and operation in particular are built upon SS7 and LTE signaling flows, which are proprietary to Tomia. I am willing to show `redacted` information to prospective employers only. Most of these products are still in use today.
