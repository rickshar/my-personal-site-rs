##  Content Conversion Project (Microsoft Word to DITA)

During my stay at Forescout, we converted our content from Microsoft Word to DITA. I played a part in the following tasks:

* Selecting a target documentation tool and platform: I presented and tested several candidate tools to the team. We decided to go with [Herreto](https://www.heretto.com/ "Herreto") (previously known as EasyDita). We liked the user-friendly interface, the native (not-proprietary) DITA, and smooth integration with our [Zoomin](https://docs.zoominsoftware.com/bundle/ZP/page/zoomin_platform.html "Zoomin Platform") documentation portal. Later on, we obtained licenses for [Oxygen XML Editor](https://www.oxygenxml.com/xml_editor/dita_editor.html "Oxygen XML Editor") and connected Heretto to Oxygen via a WebDAV, which enabled us to extend DITA functionality and tasks.
