# Writing Methodology

This section outlines how I approach technical writing, research, documentation planning, and content reuse.

## What This Section Covers

- How I gather information from subject matter experts and source systems
- How I structure documentation for clarity and reuse
- How I apply docs-as-code and content reuse practices
- How I balance speed, accuracy, and maintainability


drafting

Focal Point: As a technical writer, I see myself as a focal point in the information flow of the company. 
Developers and engineers design and maintain specific components of a product at the deep level, whereas technical writers need to understand the functionality of a complete product with all its component parts at a higher-level, and may be required to work on multiple products at the same time. This gives the technical writer a unique overview of a system, including capabilities across versions. If you are already working for a company for a reasonable period, you will gradually become a go-to person.

I like to get to know as many stakeholders as possible within the company for the products I work on:

* Backend Subject Matter Experts (SMEs): SMEs are the absolute authority on granular technical or operational knowledge. They producr architecture designs, code, technical requirements, and raw data. 
* Product Manager: Product Managers are the owners of the product vision, strategy, and business value. They determine what features get built, why they matter to the market, and how success is measured. They work on product roadmaps, user stories, market research, release plans. The PM is the go-to person When I need to understand the high-level use case and user persona.
* User Interface (UI) Team - presentation layer: Genreally split into two groups: Frontend Engineers and UI/UX Designers. Frontend Engineers understand the user workflows, visual components, state management on the frontend, and how user inputs translate into backend actions. The UI/UX Designer maps out the user journey, wireframes, and visual layout. As a technical writer, I want the writing style for the customer persone to be consistent in  the technical documentation and the UI microcopy.
* Sales Engineers work directly with customers, and maintain playbooks. The Sales Engineer is most likely to understand which functionalities of the product are most important to the customer, and provide deeper insights regarding the use persona. You can learn a tremendoes amount from a sales engineer.
